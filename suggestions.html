Getting started

You do not need to be a programmer to build something useful. Most tools in the archive started as a conversation with an AI assistant and a real classroom itch.

Where to build

Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or any assistant you already use. Describe the need, ask for a single-file tool, and iterate. The conversation is the workshop: what came out wrong on the first try becomes your next request, and that back-and-forth is the prompt journey you will share.

Where to host, free

GitHub Pages, Vercel, Netlify, Google Apps Script. Each gives a single HTML file a real address for free, so a colleague can open your tool from any browser. Your builds stay yours, hosted wherever you put them.

How to start small

Pick something that annoys you weekly and would save five minutes. A name picker, a timer, a comment starter, a sign-out sheet. Small and real beats ambitious and abandoned — you can always grow a tool that already works.
