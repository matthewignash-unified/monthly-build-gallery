The Monthly Build: This Month's Challenge

How a month runs

  1. Week 1

    The challenge drops: a real classroom need and a short intro from the month's host.

  2. Weeks 1–3

    Build window: make your tool with your AI assistant, iterating as you test it in class.

  3. Week 4

    Submissions close and finished builds join the archive.

  4. Final week

    A 60-minute recorded share-out: see what everyone built and hear their prompt journeys.

Every submission has four parts

The need

The classroom problem you set out to solve, in plain words.

The tool

A link to your working build that anyone can try.

The prompt journey

What you asked, what went wrong, and what you changed.

The code

Your repo, openly licensed so other teachers can remix it.

Community norms

  • Start with a real need. Every build begins with something genuinely worth fixing in your classroom, not a tool in search of a problem.
  • No student data, ever. Build, test, and demo with invented names only — nothing about real students goes into a tool or a submission.
  • Share the journey, not just the tool. The failed prompts and rewrites are what other teachers learn from most, so tell that part honestly.
  • Remix with credit. Every build is openly licensed. Adapting someone's tool is a compliment — name them when you do.
  • Kind, specific feedback. Critique the tool, celebrate the builder, and say what would make it work in your room.
